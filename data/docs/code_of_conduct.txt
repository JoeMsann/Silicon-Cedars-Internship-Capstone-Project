Company Code of Conduct

Our company expects every employee to conduct themselves ethically and professionally. This includes treating colleagues and clients with respect, maintaining honest communication, and following laws and company policies. Conflicts of interest should be disclosed promptly.

Dress code and workplace behavior standards promote a professional environment. Substance abuse on company premises or during work hours is strictly prohibited. Confidential information must be safeguarded and not shared outside authorized channels.

Harassment, discrimination, or bullying in any form will not be tolerated. Employees are encouraged to report any unethical or inappropriate behavior through the proper internal channels without fear of reprisal.

Compliance with this Code of Conduct ensures a positive, respectful, and productive workplace where all employees can thrive.
