IT Incident Response Plan

Purpose: This plan outlines the procedures to identify, manage, and mitigate IT security incidents to minimize impact on business operations.

    Detection & Identification: Employees or monitoring systems should promptly report any unusual activity or suspected breaches to the IT security team via the 24/7 incident hotline or ticketing system.

    Containment: The IT security team will isolate affected systems to prevent further compromise. Depending on the incident, this may involve disconnecting devices from the network or applying temporary restrictions.

    Eradication: Investigate the root cause and remove malicious code, unauthorized access points, or vulnerabilities. Verify that systems are clean before resuming normal operations.

    Recovery: Restore systems from secure backups and monitor closely for signs of recurring issues. Communicate incident status and resolution progress to relevant stakeholders.

    Post-Incident Review: Conduct a thorough analysis of the incident, document lessons learned, and update policies or safeguards accordingly to prevent future recurrence.

The incident response team consists of designated personnel from IT, security, legal, and communications departments with clearly assigned roles for efficient coordination.
